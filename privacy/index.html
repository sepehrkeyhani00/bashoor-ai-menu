AI connection privacy

This notice covers Bashoor’s public, read-only MCP endpoint and the public menu mirror. It does not cover reservations, staff systems or third-party AI services.

The connection requires no account and exposes public business and menu information. Menu searches send the search text, optional category, budget and result limit to Bashoor’s server. Do not send personal, payment or sensitive information in a search.

Web-server operational logs may contain IP address, request time, requested path, response status and user-agent. The menu tools do not require your conversation history, email, name or payment details. Your AI provider determines what it sends to the endpoint and handles its own conversation data.

The public mirror is hosted by GitHub Pages, which has its own hosting and privacy practices. OpenAI, Google and other clients have separate policies. For privacy questions or requests concerning Bashoor, use the contact details on Bashoor’s official contact page.

Scope: public AI connection. Updated 11 October 2026.
